## Production rules
- Transparent background unless asset is explicitly a background
- No checkerboard baked into pixels
- No collage/sheet/sprite
- No explanatory text inside icons
- Mascot geometry must stay consistent across all states
- Render at 2x mobile target size minimum
- Optimize PNG after approval
- Every file must be previewed against #070914 before shipping
